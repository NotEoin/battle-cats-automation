Battle Cats Screen Map

The map the bot navigates by. It never trusts a remembered position. At every step it identifies the screen from the frame itself, by its title and the text and shapes that must or must not be on it, then takes the first hop of the fewest-taps route to its goal. A screen it can't place gets the back arrow.
